Any sensor in.
The whole plant out.

Inverse procedural modeling of occluded row crop geometry from sparse multimodal field observations.

One masked autoencoder reads any field sensor (an image, a depth image or a point cloud) and writes the procedural model of the plant, even when its neighbours hide part of it. Every 3D view on this page is a held-out test plant: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset.

Regime II · partial point clouds

One camera, three cameras, or the whole plant

The headline model gets the image and depth image of camera 00 and a point cloud from one camera, three cameras, or the full plant, and reconstructs the plant. Sorghum: the points of the complete plant that each camera sees; its model trained on complete plants, so more cameras help. Maize: the clouds of the cameras fused (full = all ten); its model trained on single-camera clouds, so one camera is its home ground and fused clouds are new to it, as in the paper's table.
