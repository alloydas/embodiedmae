CropCode

Inverse procedural modeling of occluded row crop geometry from sparse multimodal field observations

One masked autoencoder reads any field sensor (an image, a depth image or a point cloud) and writes the procedural model of the plant, even when its neighbours hide part of it. Every 3D view on this page is a held-out test plant; drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset.

Isolated synthetic plants (15,000 per species, ten cameras each), synthetic crop rows where two neighbours occlude the center plant, and rows built from real plant models (maize) and scans (sorghum).
