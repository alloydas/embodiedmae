Reconstruction

Point-cloud precision, recall and F1

Each plant's view-0 cloud is reconstructed in the run's own training regime (Dirichlet masking at mask ratio 0.80, the recipe hidden and zeroed, as in the trainer's validation pass), then compared point by point with a second seeded subsample of the complete cloud. Both are in the loader's frame: centred, farthest point on the unit sphere. At a distance threshold τ:

  • precision = share of predicted points within τ of some ground-truth point (no stray points),
  • recall = share of ground-truth points within τ of some predicted point (nothing missing),
  • F1 = 2PR / (P + R), computed per plant and averaged over plants.

A sorghum cloud spans about 1 m from its centre, so τ = 0.01 is about 1 cm; τ = 0.01 is the headline.

Decision 6.4

Linear probe on the frozen CLS token

Ridge regression on the CLS token, one deterministic view per plant, recipe never visible and parameters zeroed, scaler fit on train only, every model served the RGB brightness it trained at. R² on the held-out split. Height, leaf count and biomass are one generator variable in sorghum (r ≈ 0.99); roll, leaf length and leaf width are the independent axes. Waviness is at R² ≈ 0 for every arm and is omitted.

Significance

Paired bootstrap of the probe

Δ = R²(a) − R²(b), 2,000 resamples over plants, 95 % interval. A win or loss needs the interval to exclude zero on both val and test; everything else is a draw.

Distillation

Single-sensor generation

Teacher = student init = E1 (epoch 1,000). Nine distillation runs in total: 5 running, 4 queued. The generalist was preempted on scavenger at epoch 47 and is queued on nova (it resumes from epoch 46). The generalist sees one source at a time and regenerates all four streams. Three single-stream students map RGB → point cloud. Four two-stream students (added 10-23) map a pair of streams → point cloud: RGB + depth (an RGB-D camera), RGB + recipe, depth + recipe, and one student trained on all three pairs. Pairs that already contain the point cloud are not distilled, since their output is one of their inputs. Every student uses the RGB student's recipe (lr 3 × 10⁻⁵, 100 epochs, global batch 128), so the two-stream rows compare directly with the RGB-only one. Before = E1 used zero-shot; after = the distilled student.

Cross-species

One species' encoder on the other species' plants

PLAN_cross_species.md, Z1. A frozen encoder trained on one species is probed on the other species' plants with that species' own targets, splits and ridge protocol. The recipe is never visible; depth is re-encoded from the data's render convention into the model's (species_conventions.py), RGB is served as the data ships it. Val R²; the native rows are the data species' own models through its own probe.
