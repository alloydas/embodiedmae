Maize rows, seed 1

The two zips in /work/mech-ai/alloydas, unpacked. Each row is one of 520 maize plant models repeated along a row; a camera 1 m to the side stops 18 times along it and saves a photograph and a labelled point cloud at every stop. The leaf planes run along the row in one layout and across it in the other.
