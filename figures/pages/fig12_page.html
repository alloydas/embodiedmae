CVPR 28592 · draft figures in the layout of SAGA (CVPR 2026)

Figures 1 and 2, rebuilt as editable draw.io

Both figures follow SAGA's structure: a three-panel teaser, and a method figure with stacked training stages and a zoom-in on the network. Every box, label and arrow is a native draw.io shape; the plant tiles, plots and charts are embedded images.

Figure 1 · overview teaser

On a phone, scroll the figure sideways.

(a) OverviewThe current teaser's held-out test rows: image, depth image and scene point cloud in; the center plant's procedural parameters, generated point cloud and traits out. The CropCode box shows its four internal steps, as SAGA's framework box does.
(b) Latent spacet-SNE of the headline models' mean-pooled features, 2,250 validation plants per species, coloured by leaf count (sorghum) and height (maize). It shows a smooth trait gradient, not clusters: there are no genotypes to cluster.
(c) Trait probe radarThe nine trait columns of Table 10 (validation R²). Each axis is scaled from the lowest to the highest model; CropCode's value is printed at each axis. The supervised reference is drawn dashed.

Figure 2 · training stages

Stage 1 · pretrainingOne held-out sorghum test plant: its four streams, what the headline model sees at mask ratio 0.8 (visible tokens 37 / 11 / 56 / 18), and its reconstruction. Losses run back along the red dashed line.
Stage 2 · distillationFrozen teacher with all four streams, student with one. The cloud is the distilled generalist's output from the image alone. Bars: single-stream Chamfer before and after distillation (Table 6).
Stage 3 · rowsA crop row in, the center plant out. Bars: Chamfer on rows for the zero-shot, isolated-trained and row fine-tuned models (Table 5). Stages 2 and 3 both warm-start from Stage 1, as the runs did.
Inside CropCodeTokenizers per stream, the Dirichlet split of the visible budget, the ViT-B encoder and decoder, and the four output heads.

Check before these go in the paper

Space. Figure 1 takes about a quarter of a page and Figure 2 about 40%. The main-versus-supplement cut on Thursday decides whether both fit.
The radar's claim. CropCode leads on 6 of 9 axes; the supervised network leads on the other three: sorghum branch angle, maize height and maize stem radius. The figure shows that honestly, so it reads as "best without labels", not "best everywhere".
