\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\upd{\CropCode{} recovers a complete, editable procedural plant from the data of
whichever sensor a field campaign used, in one forward pass, by using the
generator for both the training data and the output representation.} \upd{Two
findings from its ablations apply beyond plants: the generator's parameters
harm the representation when most of them are visible to the encoder, and on
slender geometry a
downstream probe, not Chamfer distance, should decide what a modality is worth.} \upd{A single camera's partial view requires refitting the readout and leaves a
measured transfer gap; the row-occluded and real-scan evaluations will show
whether the generator's prior covers the remaining difference to field data.} \todo{Sharpen once E10 lands.}
