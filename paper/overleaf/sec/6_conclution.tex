\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\CropCode{} recovers a complete, editable procedural plant from whichever
sensor a field campaign produced, in one forward pass, by letting the generator
supply both the training data and the output representation. Two findings
from its ablations should outlast the application: the generator's parameters
are valuable targets but harmful encoder inputs, and on slender
geometry a downstream probe, not Chamfer distance, is the metric that decides
what a modality is worth. The remaining distance to the field is measured, not
guessed: a single camera's partial view costs a refit readout and a known
transfer gap, and the row-occluded and real-scan evaluations will say whether
the generator's prior pays for the rest. \todo{Sharpen once E10 lands.}
