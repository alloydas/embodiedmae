What the 4-modality masked autoencoder sees at epoch {{EPOCH}}

One encoder is shown a small, randomly chosen slice of a sorghum plant — a few RGB patches, a few depth patches, a handful of point-cloud groups, some growth parameters — and asked to put the whole plant back together in all four modalities at once. These plates compare what it produces against ground truth on plants it has never been trained on.

Everything below comes from a single pinned checkpoint at epoch {{EPOCH}}, evaluated on the held-out test split — plants held out by plant, so no view of them appears in training. The split is deliberately enriched for morphological extremes, so these are harder than average plants. Masking is the same {{MASK_RATIO}} total ratio used during training, and the mask is seeded identically wherever two panels are meant to be compared.

Two things to read carefully. The RGB head is trained with norm_pix_loss, which divides each patch's mean and variance out of the target — so the head never learns them, and every RGB reconstruction here has the target's per-patch mean and variance restored before display. Judge structure in those panels, not absolute colour. And the loader subsamples each cloud to 8,196 points with a fresh draw per run, so a single plant's Chamfer carries a few percent of sampling noise — the aggregate figures over many plants are the ones to quote.
